Question (Research Objective)

Research Question: Does equipping the AK-47 produce a statistically significant increase in Average Damage per Round (ADR) during T-side "Full-Buy" rounds (>$3,900) compared to equipping the Galil AR in Premier Counter-Strike 2 matches?

Operational Definition: Round Damage (HP) is total health-point damage dealt to enemy players in a T-side full-buy round (0 to 500 HP). Full-Buy = >$3,900 start money (excluding pistol, eco, and force-buy rounds).

Procedure

Structural Choice: Randomized Comparative Experiment.

Treatment Assignment: Before each Premier match, a coin flip determined assigned weapon (Heads = AK-47, Tails = Galil AR).

Control Protocol: Buy only the assigned rifle on T-side full-buy rounds (>$3,900). Play normally on pistol/eco rounds. Matches with accidental wrong-weapon buys or early forfeits are discarded.

Hypothetical Sampling Paths (Prompt Requirements):

  • SRS: Select 50 round IDs out of 500 historical rounds using `randInt(1, 500)` without replacement.
  • Stratified: Group rounds by Map (Mirage, Nuke, Inferno) and randomly sample 10 rounds per map.
  • Cluster: Treat Premier matches as clusters, randomly select 5 matches, and record all full-buy rounds within them.
  • Systematic: Select every 5th T-side full-buy round chronologically after a random start between 1 and 5.
Population vs. Sample

Population: All T-side full-buy competitive rounds played under standardized Premier CS2 match conditions.

Sample: n = 73 T-side full-buy rounds collected across 10 completed Premier matches (30 AK-47 rounds, 43 Galil AR rounds).

Scope of Inference: Treatments were randomly assigned via coin flip, allowing causal inference regarding weapon choice and round damage output. However, because the player was not randomly selected from all CS2 players, results cannot be generalized to the broader player base.

Hypothesis
  • Null Hypothesis (H0): μAK - μGalil = 0 (No difference in true mean round damage between AK-47 and Galil AR).
  • Alternative Hypothesis (Ha): μAK - μGalil > 0 (The AK-47 yields a higher true mean round damage than the Galil AR due to its one-tap headshot capability).
Possible Confounding Variable
  • Map Geometry / Side Disparity: Maps like Nuke feature tighter choke points than Inferno or Mirage, altering engagement distances where Galil damage drop-off occurs.
  • Matchmaking Variance: Skill levels of opposing players and teammate utility setups vary match-to-match, introducing noise into round damage.
Possible Source of Bias
  • Selection / Sampling Bias: Mitigated by pre-match coin flip randomization for treatment assignment.
  • Economy Disparity (Non-Response / Execution Error): Early match forfeits or blowout matches (e.g., 5–13 loss with only 2 full-buy rounds) create extreme economic distortion; handled via protocol discard rules.
