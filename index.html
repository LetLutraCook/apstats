An Empirical Comparison of AK-47 vs. Galil AR Performance in CS2

AP Statistics Final Poster Board Blueprint & Layout Guide

1

Introduction

Research Question

Does equipping the AK-47 produce a statistically significant increase in Average Damage per Round (ADR) during T-side "Full-Buy" rounds (>$3,900) compared to equipping the Galil AR in Premier Counter-Strike 2 matches?

Hypotheses
  • H0: μAK - μGalil = 0 (No difference in true mean round damage)
  • Ha: μAK - μGalil > 0 (AK-47 yields higher true mean round damage)
Operational Definitions
  • T-side Full-Buy Round: T-side round with >$3,900 start money allowing full primary rifle purchase (excluding pistol/eco/force-buys).
  • Round Damage (HP): Total health-point damage dealt to enemy players in a full-buy round (0 to 500 HP).
Background Rationale

CS2 economy requires choosing between saving for an AK-47 ($2,700) or buying a Galil AR ($1,800) to afford full utility. Quantifying round damage output provides an empirical basis for eco-planning.

2

Data Collection & Design

Variables
  • Explanatory (Categorical): Assigned Rifle (AK-47 vs. Galil AR).
  • Response (Quantitative): Damage dealt per T-side full-buy round (HP).
Structural Choice

Randomized Comparative Experiment. Treatments (AK-47 vs. Galil AR) were assigned per match via pre-game coin flip. Blocked on >$3,900 buy-type to eliminate pistol/eco noise.

Hypothetical Sampling Paths
  • SRS: Select 50 round IDs from 500 historical rounds using a random number generator (`randInt(1,500)` without replacement).
  • Stratified: Group rounds by Map (Mirage, Nuke, Inferno) and randomly sample 10 rounds per map.
  • Cluster: Treat Premier matches as clusters, randomly select 5 matches, and record all full-buy rounds within them.
  • Systematic: Select every 5th T-side full-buy round chronologically after a random start between 1 and 5.
Bias Evaluation

Selection bias controlled via pre-match random assignment. Map geometry and opponent armor tier act as potential confounders across matches.

Formal Inference Statement

Because weapon treatments were randomly assigned via coin flip, we can establish causal inference regarding rifle choice and damage output under these experimental conditions. However, because the player was not randomly selected from all CS2 players, findings cannot be generalized to the broader player base.

6

Statistical Reflection

Process Critique & Execution

An initial 5–13 blowout match (Game 10) produced a severe low-end outlier (2 full-buy rounds, 42.5 ADR) due to economy starvation. Per protocol, matches failing execution integrity were discarded and re-run to maintain valid full-buy data.

Hidden Confounders

Map choke geometries (e.g., Nuke vs. Inferno) and matchmaking skill variance across lobbies created uncontrolled noise in engagement ranges and enemy armor context.

Future Procedural Changes

Future studies should block by Map prior to treatment randomization and subtract utility damage (HE/Molotov) to isolate purely rifle-inflicted HP.
